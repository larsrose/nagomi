About

Nagomi is an (ad)venture founded by Lars Rosengren, aspiring to become a not-for-profit cooperative. We are a think tank and an agency of sorts, but also hope to detach from such labels, as they carry legacy of a different time period.

We live in a world where our leaders don’t lead with example, and we need new perspectives to develop active hope for the future.

Nagomi recognises the paradox of living and operating within a system that co-opts us to be in conflict with our visions, Yet, we must embrace paradox, operate within the system, while resisting conforming to it.

We are always open to cooperation and partnerships, so please get in touch!
